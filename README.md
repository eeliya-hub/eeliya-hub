### Hi, I'm Eeliya.

I design and build **[Pulse OS](https://github.com/eeliya-hub/pulseOS)**, a
personal command centre that puts your whole day on one calm screen — your
plans, the weather, the news, the markets, your music and your travel — with an
assistant that can read all of it and act on it. Type to it, or just talk.

It now has a launch film: two and a half minutes on every part of the app, every
frame of it drawn in code, and a thirty-second ad made for phones.

**Inside Pulse OS**

- A living sky that follows the hour, and takes the colours of whatever is playing
- Google and iCloud calendars, read and written side by side
- An assistant on Gemini, OpenAI or Claude, with real-time voice
- Spotify built in, with a full-screen player lit by the record
- Live flight tracking, maps and currency for every trip

**Built with** React, Vite, Express, the Gemini Live API, the Spotify Web
Playback SDK and MapLibre — and, for the films, Remotion.
